# Corner Kitchen Cookbook: Pasta Basics

## 1. Boiling

1.1 Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil before adding the pasta. Stir during the first minute so the pieces do not stick together.

## 2. Tomato sauce

2.1 Fry chopped garlic gently in olive oil, add crushed tomatoes and simmer for twenty minutes. Season with basil and a pinch of sugar.

2.2 Toss the drained pasta in the sauce with a spoonful of the cooking water so the sauce clings.
