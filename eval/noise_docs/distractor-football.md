# Eastgate Youth League: Match Day Rules

## 1. Team size

1.1 Each side fields seven players, including the goalkeeper. Substitutes may enter at any stoppage with the referee's permission.

## 2. Match length

2.1 A match consists of two halves of twenty minutes with a five minute break. The referee may add time for injuries.

## 3. Throw-ins

3.1 A throw-in is taken with both hands from behind the head. The ball must be released from the point where it left the pitch.
