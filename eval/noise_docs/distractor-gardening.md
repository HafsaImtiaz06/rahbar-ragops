# Riverside Allotment Society: Growing Guide

## 1. Sowing tomatoes

1.1 Sow tomato seeds indoors in trays six weeks before the last frost. Keep the trays on a warm windowsill and water lightly every second day.

1.2 Move seedlings outdoors only after a week of hardening off. Plant them deep, up to the first true leaves, and tie each stem to a cane.

## 2. Composting

2.1 A good compost heap needs equal parts green material, such as vegetable peelings, and brown material, such as cardboard and dry leaves. Turn the heap once a month.
