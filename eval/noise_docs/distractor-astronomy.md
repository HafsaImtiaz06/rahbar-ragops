# Hilltop Observatory Club: Beginner Notes

## 1. Choosing a telescope

1.1 A reflector telescope gathers more light for its price than a refractor of the same size. Beginners usually start with a six-inch reflector on a simple mount.

## 2. Observing planets

2.1 Jupiter and Saturn are best observed when they are opposite the Sun in the sky. Allow the telescope to cool outdoors for half an hour before looking.

2.2 Use a low-power eyepiece to find the planet, then switch to a higher power to see cloud bands and rings.
