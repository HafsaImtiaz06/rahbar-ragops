# Fee Information (Prospectus 2027)

> Synthetic test document created for the CS4032 RAGOps assignment (Batch B). It is not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

## 1. Tuition
Tuition for undergraduate programs is Rs 42,500 per credit hour. The fee for a semester is calculated from the number of credit hours the student registers for.

## 2. Late payment
A fee voucher that is paid after its due date carries a late fee of Rs 1,000 per day, up to a maximum of Rs 15,000 for the semester.

## 3. Refunds
A student who withdraws from the university within the first week of the semester receives a refund of 80 percent of the tuition. No refund is given after the second week.

## 4. Installments
A student may pay the semester fee in two installments. The second installment is due before the mid-term examinations.
