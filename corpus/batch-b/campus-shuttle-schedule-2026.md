# Campus Shuttle Schedule 2026

> Synthetic test document created for the CS4032 RAGOps assignment (Batch B). It is not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

## 1. Morning routes
Route A runs from Saddar Metro Station to the main gate and leaves Saddar at 7:15 and 8:15 in the morning. Route B runs from Faizabad to the main gate and leaves at 7:30 and 8:30.

## 2. Return trips
Return buses leave the main gate at 1:30, 3:30 and 5:30 in the afternoon. On Fridays the last return bus leaves at 4:30.

## 3. Fares
Travel is free for students who show a valid student card. A student who forgets the card pays Rs 100 for that day, and a guest pays Rs 150 per trip.

## 4. Changes
The transport office announces any change to the timetable on the notice board at least two days in advance.
