# Library Rules 2026

> Synthetic test document created for the CS4032 RAGOps assignment (Batch B). It is not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

## 1. Opening hours
The central library is open from 8:00 in the morning until 10:00 at night, Monday to Friday, and from 9:00 in the morning until 4:00 in the afternoon on Saturday. It is closed on Sunday and on public holidays.

## 2. Borrowing
An undergraduate student may borrow up to six books at one time for fourteen days. A book may be renewed twice, unless another member has reserved it. Reference books and past examination papers may not leave the reading room.

## 3. Fines
An overdue book carries a fine of Rs 20 per day. A student whose unpaid library fines exceed Rs 500 cannot register for the next semester until the fines are cleared at the library desk.

## 4. Lost books
A lost book must be replaced with the same edition or paid for at its current price plus a processing charge of Rs 300.
